homeWatch maps how your devices are connected, using switch MAC tables, UniFi clients, Home Assistant and Proxmox. When a problem traces back to an upstream device that’s down, restarting or has lost its link, homeWatch points to that device as the likely cause. It sends one alert for that device and lists the affected devices behind it when the incident is over.
