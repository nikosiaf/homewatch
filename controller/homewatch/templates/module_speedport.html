Before you switch it on:
  • The Speedport has only its admin login, so homewatch needs that password. It is stored encrypted and never shown again.
  • Each reading logs in, which ends any open browser session on the modem's web page. Keep the interval at 15 minutes or more.
  • After a rejected login the modem slows down further attempts, so homewatch waits 6 hours (or until you save new credentials).
  • The host running homewatch must be able to reach the modem's address. Behind a separate router or firewall this may need a rule there.
